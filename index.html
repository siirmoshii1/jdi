Core System Modules & Scope Breakdown

Comprehensive audit of all 11 core sub-systems within the JDI Platform, outlining active features, verification status, and module functions.

PURCHASES
For Testing
  • Purchase Order (PO) Create & encode purchase orders to suppliers with item quantities, purchase costs, payment terms, and Days of Inventory (DOI) recommendations.

Core Engine
  • Master Stock Matrix Real-time central matrix showing all products, warehouse on-hand quantities, and inventory values.

Purchases Module for Testing Procurement

Purchase Order (PO) User Guide

Learn how to create, encode, and print purchase orders to suppliers, configure Days of Inventory (DOI) recommendations, and utilize the automated reorder planner.

STEP 01

Accessing the Purchases Module

From the JDI System main home dashboard, locate and click on the Purchases option on the main navigation menu.

This action will redirect you to the Purchases portal where you can access the various modules relating to procurement, orders, and supplier fulfillment.

Figure 1.1: Home Dashboard — Selecting Purchases Menu
Click to zoom
STEP 02

Selecting Supplier & Purchase Order Header

You will automatically be redirected to the Purchase Order module. You may also navigate between purchasing submodules using the top navigation bar.

Select which supplier to create an order for by clicking on the supplier name in the directory. This opens the Purchase Header Modal.

Purchase Header Modal Fields:
  • Supplier Info: Displays selected supplier, registered contact person, and address.
  • Warehouse & Location: Choose the destination warehouse where supplier deliveries will be received.
  • Payment Terms: Specify invoice credit terms (e.g. 30 Days, COD).
  • Days of Inventory (DOI): Input your desired DOI to calculate system reorder recommendations.
  • Remarks: Optional notes or special instructions for the vendor.
Click Save once finalized to proceed to item encoding.

STEP 03

Adding Products & Specifying Quantities

From the product catalog list, browse available items and click on the + Add button on any product row.

Clicking + Add opens a dedicated quantity modal where you input the ordered quantity. Once entered, click Save. You can repeat this process to add multiple SKUs to the order.

STEP 04

Reviewing Order, Reorder Planner, Finalizing & Printing

All selected products are compiled into an order summary table showing quantities, unit costs, and sub-totals. An Edit button on the right allows adjusting quantities if necessary.

Reorder Planner Feature: Click on the Reorder Planner button to automatically evaluate sales velocity against your desired DOI and auto-populate recommended purchase quantities.

Once verified, click Save Order. You can then click the Print button to generate the official Purchase Order document.

Step-by-step workflow for verifying incoming supplier shipments, recording delivered quantities, assigning warehouse bin locations, updating batch expiration dates, and printing the Receiving Receipt (RR).

STEP 01

Navigating to Purchase Receiving

From the Purchases section, click on the Purchase Receiving tab on the top navigation bar to open the pending receiving receipts queue.

STEP 02

Encoding Received Qty, Bin Location & Expiry Date

Click the Edit button next to any product on the ordered list. This opens the receiving detail modal:

  • Ordered Quantity: Reference count showing what was initially requested.
  • Qty Received: Enter the exact physical quantity delivered by the supplier.

Products Module for Testing Master Catalog

Products Master Catalog User Guide

Create new SKUs with automatic markup and loose price calculations, edit existing products, queue scheduled price changes, and export catalogs to Excel.

STEP 01

Adding & Registering New Products

Select Products on the home dashboard. To register a new SKU, click on the + Add New Product button. This opens the Create Product Modal:

  • Description & Product Code: Unique SKU identifier and full name.
  • Barcodes: Loose Barcode (single unit) and Case Barcode.
  • Conversion Unit & Product Size: Number of loose pieces per case.
  • Vatable Toggle: Enable or disable VAT application.
  • Case Bare Cost & Case Markup: Cost basis and markup percentage.
  • Auto-Calculations: Case Price and Loose Price are automatically computed from Case Bare Cost, Markup, and Conversion Unit.
  • Supplier: Assign the primary supplying vendor.

Click Create to register the product into the master database.

STEP 03

Scheduling Automated Future Price Changes

To schedule a future price change without disrupting current billing, click on the Scheduled Price Changes button.

How to Deploy This Manual Online

This documentation is built as a 100% static, zero-dependency HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript portal. You can deploy it anywhere in under 1 minute.

Deployment Options:

  • Local WAMP Server: Already configured and live right now at http://localhost/jtcinv-jm/manual/.
  • GitHub Pages: Push the manual/ folder to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages in repository settings under Settings → Pages.
  • Netlify / Vercel: Drag and drop the c:\wamp64\www\jtcinv-jm\manual folder onto the Netlify drop zone or connect your repository.
  • Cloudflare Pages: Connect to your Git repository or upload the static assets folder.
  • Apache / Nginx Web Server: Upload the folder directly to any /var/www/html/ or public_html/ directory.
